Our Story - Kopanong

More than food - we are family | The Gathering Place

From Fruits to Famous Kotas

2004: Established by our father as a fruits and vegetables business.

2006: Handed to his wife, who expanded to fresh produce + cooked plates.

2010: Passed to daughter, current owner. Introduced kotas - now our signature.

Today Kopanong Restaurant is known for affordable, filling meals and strong community love in Brixton, Johannesburg.
